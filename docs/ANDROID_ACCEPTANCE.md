# HamZa Android acceptance and release gate

Do not dispatch an APK build until the agreed functional scope has been reviewed and accepted for device validation. The reference parity document contains candidates, not a promise to implement every proprietary workflow. An online account system, cloud sync, advanced tax rules and hardware integration are not prerequisites for the offline HamZa edition unless separately agreed.

## Before the first candidate build

- Confirm the exact candidate SHA on the completion branch. Run domain tests, TypeScript, the literal translation audit and Android JS bundling at that SHA. A JavaScript bundle is not an APK.
- Check the default-branch workflow is discoverable for a manual dispatch and that the dispatch chooses the completion branch. Do not merge to main solely to trigger a build.
- The workflow requires KVM read/write before boot and checks that the exact APK packages x86_64 before attempting the x86_64 smoke test; release config also retains both ARM phone ABIs. These changes are configured, not runner-verified until the first authorized candidate. [GitHub hosted-runner KVM support](https://github.blog/changelog/2024-04-02-github-actions-hardware-accelerated-android-virtualization-now-available/).
- Replace the per-run temporary signing key before handing out a new release. The APK shared on November 8 had a temporary signing certificate with SHA-256 `78b3733153c4107730bd1822e2f8d82c293fb9eb9c10375e681735c5f5adedf8` (public digest, not a key). This identifies one artifact, not the APK actually installed on the user's device. Use a stable protected keystore, never a Git commit or build artifact for key material. Confirm package `com.hamza.app`, signer digest and a nondecreasing versionCode; test update without clearing state when the existing signer is available. A mismatched signer is not a reason to uninstall an app with unverified user data.

## Private release route on the public repository

The completion-branch job uses the `hamza-android-release` environment. Restrict it to `soxo9/hamza-completion` before provisioning anything. Configure these environment secrets: `HAMZA_RELEASE_KEYSTORE_B64` (PKCS#12), `HAMZA_RELEASE_STORE_PASSWORD`, and `HAMZA_RELEASE_KEY_PASSWORD`. Configure the non-secret environment variables `HAMZA_RELEASE_KEY_ALIAS`, `HAMZA_RELEASE_SIGNER_SHA256` (64 hex digits), and `HAMZA_APK_RECIPIENT_CERT_B64` (Base64-encoded DER of an RSA recipient certificate). The certificate's private decryption key stays outside Actions and source control. Verify the recipient before building, and ensure the owner can recover the stable signing key from protected storage. Do not paste key material into chat, source, logs or artifacts.

The separate Gradle step builds unsigned with no signing secrets. The next step makes a temporary protected copy of the key, signs, verifies the exact designated signer and 16 KB ZIP alignment, and removes the key before Android testing. After installation and smoke tests, a digest check protects the exact signed bytes; only the authenticated encrypted CMS file is uploaded. The completion branch does not publish the APK, decompiled contents or an unencrypted archive. The branch's previous public artifact layout is not a private handoff.

Decrypt in an authorized private workspace with OpenSSL CMS, verify the expected archive members before extracting them, check the APK digest and certificate locally, and verify the encrypted run and source SHA match the accepted build. Then attach the raw APK privately. Track the exact version code, any update/signer incompatibility and real-device checks separately. Provisioning and a real Actions run have not yet been verified.

## Historical data compatibility gate

- The previous APKs built on Nov 10 (commits `3c525e07abbeb194de447bf5d29ad11cbc8f392a` and `cdf95acfeb098b83df30c108e39bc999e394d19f`) used the old chunks-v1 pointer and exported regular HamZa JSON. Their public signer digests are `4cc166b69bb750f133363aa2b565eb1b3465aabf25fe44276b239afec0f517d4` and `2f96ca22b827822a47b19e53a1be784e625eb6516949c331f61256315bdcbf99`; neither proves the user's installed version.
- A compatibility reader and verified two-slot file writes are in place. Reading and committing a migrated copy cannot overwrite the historical root/chunk keys. Unknown historical fields remain in the snapshot and the JSON export. The safe adapter covers legacy warehouse stock (including signed stock counts and transfers), photo and contact field names, sale cost, and supplier purchase totals with linked cheques, cash and proven bank settlements reconciled without double counting. The independent historical cash journal carries its exact balance and transaction list forward; only pre-existing generated cash-row IDs are suppressed, and new transactions must change the balance normally. Legacy bank balances come from the original signed account movements, with exact matching for single and split supplier payments and both sides of transfers. Reconciliation evidence is retained without reapplying its adjustment. Historical customer returns preserve the original cents and already-booked stock; the old customer-level credit pool is materialized against old invoice order at cutover, without a second stock change, payment or refund. Old supplier invoices are visible as a read-only financial history. When terminal order, receipt, same-price invoice and payment evidence agree, their settlements reduce the linked purchase debt without duplicating the invoice liability, bank/cash journal entries or stock. Unmatched or still-active legacy orders stay in protected recovery. Verified historical supplier returns are noncash credits: first offset their own purchase, then other purchases and opening debt at the same supplier. Excess remains visible as available supplier credit. The source stock movement is not replayed. Invalid cash or bank activity, unmatched returns, variants and other material old records intentionally cause recovery-only mode. A raw verified export remains available there; original records are not modified. Do not equate this with field-level compatibility. Before shipping, verify the supported historical cases on Android and reconcile subsidiary returns; the raw export alone is not proof of migration. Resolve or explicitly protect unsupported legacy financial state before representing balances as correct.
- Core storage mocks exercise broken old chunk pointers, failed new writes and pointer swaps, and portable business-data JSON. Real Android filesystem/SQLite, actual attachment copies and upgrades remain unverified.

## Agreed-scope source audit

The completion branch has runnable screens and domain coverage for the agreed offline app: Arabic/French black-and-gold UI; product entry/edit, photos, barcode/reference search, voice and bulk import; client/supplier records; sales, purchases, balances, expenses and stock; point of sale; projects/agenda; and matching reporting/export paths. Devis, facture, bon de sortie and avoir support the agreed MAD TTC and with/without-photos exports. The main menu's module titles all map to real screens. This is a source review, not a declaration that every workflow has passed on Android. Do not gate this APK on unrelated reference features such as an online role system, cloud backup, an advanced tax engine or vendor integrations.

The CI native smoke starts clean on a disposable emulator, tests the exact signed package with Wi-Fi and data off, saves and reloads a product and French UI choice, finds the product in POS, rejects adding beyond stock, and parks and resumes the cart across app restart. It returns to Arabic. It does not prove native camera/voice capture, file picker, final commercial PDF rendering/print, full business flow or an upgrade from the user's unknown prior signer; run the acceptance checks below with disposable data.

## Candidate checks on Android, with disposable business records

1. Install the exact candidate, launch with Metro off and network disabled, change French to Arabic and back, and relaunch. Review navigation, input order, mirroring, digits, line clipping and permission refusal. Test a Samsung/physical Android device for camera and local speech when available; an emulator with no compatible recognizer is not proof of recognition.
2. Add a photo and barcode to a product, import a UTF-8 CSV via the file picker, reject a duplicate, and check the selected warehouse. Run POS and standalone invoice, including quote → delivery → invoice and the inverse order; check warehouse quantities, paid/credited amounts and the ledger. Return a line and reconcile stock, credit and margin. In POS sell three 10 DH products with a 0.01 DH discount; invoice and full-return credits must total exactly 29.99 DH. Try a 50 DH cash refund with 100 DH customer credit but only 20 DH in the till (reject; no posting), then refund 20 DH (80 DH credit, 0 DH in the till and one journal posting after restart). Also try to delete a product with four units valued at 40 DH (blocked; no report or ledger change), then a sold-out product with a recorded sale (blocked; a restocked return restores one unit after relaunch). An unused zero-stock product should still delete after confirmation. Type 18,75 and 2,5 into an already added quote line, repeat with ١٨٫٧٥ and ٢٫٥, and add a 20.00 DH line with a 5.00 DH whole-document discount. Verify 46.88 + 20.00 = 66.88, minus 5.00 = 61.88 DH after reopening and in the quote/direct or converted invoice PDF, with and without photos. Editor drafts must retain the separator during typing. Older records with no saved discount keep their original totals without fabricated labels.
3. Record supplier receipt, partial payment, credit, a cash expense and a bank transfer. Verify stock count adjusts only the difference. Confirm sales, purchase, stock and cash reports respond to date/search; verify warehouse/direction and row counts. For debt reports confirm contact search changes current balances and changing the report date does not rewrite them into historical balances.
4. Open PDF, CSV and XLSX from Android share. Compare report exports to on-screen values, including Arabic labels, negative movements and no-results views. Print a sample PDF. Compare Arabic/French devis, facture, bon de sortie and avoir, with and without photos. Use the six-row fixture at `tests/fixtures/bon-de-sortie.json` with the same saved date and fictitious client. In a real phone PDF viewer at fit-to-width and in print preview, check plain French with no company logo, then attach two test product photos and check the Arabic and French versions; keep the same date, all six rows, unit prices, reference, and 2 390,00 DH TTC in French, with no separate TVA line. Check that issuing the delivery and linked invoice deducts exactly 12, 1, 6, 100, 4 and 4 once.
5. Use fake local data for backup/restore and update tests. Test earlier plain JSON and the Nov 10 chunks-v1 pointer/content, then reopen the new two-slot file snapshot. Compare historical external JSON and app data with French/Arabic text, gallery images and task attachments. Confirm persistence across process restart and interruption before the pointer commit. Relaunch after operations, export a backup externally, restore it on a separate installation, and compare stock, balances, dictionaries, projects/appointments and images. Exercise invalid backup/restore and app restart; do not target the user's live records.
6. Record exact APK SHA-256, certificate, version, automated and real-device checks, and any untested native paths. Deliver only after the verified candidate is accessible to the phone user; test the actual link or native attachment. Deliver the raw APK, not just an expiring CI artifact ZIP. Verify any private handoff route before promising it.
