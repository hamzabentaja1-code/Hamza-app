# HamZa UI components

Arabic screens use right-to-left layout. French screens use left-to-right layout.

## App shell
Dashboard, documents, customers, products, stock, finance, reports and settings.

## Buttons
Primary, secondary, outline, ghost and danger variants. Include default, pressed, focused, disabled and loading states. Important actions have accessible labels and touch targets of at least 44dp.

## Form fields
Text, number, currency, phone, date, search and multiline fields. Every field has a visible label and validation message. Store MAD values as integer centimes.

## Document editor
Customer selector, searchable product picker, multiple editable lines, quantity, unit price, discount, totals and notes. Prevent saving an empty document.

## Payments
Amount, date, method, reference and notes. Show remaining balance and reject allocations greater than the outstanding amount.

## PDF preview
Arabic right-to-left and French left-to-right output, business profile, customer details, line items and totals. Verify rendering on Android.

## Accessibility
Support screen readers, Android font scaling, visible focus and scrolling on small screens.
